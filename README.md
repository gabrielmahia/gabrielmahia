# Gabriel Mahia

**Building decision infrastructure for under-resourced institutions.**

I design and ship systems that reduce opacity at the point of decision — giving people with limited time and incomplete information the clarity they need to act. The work spans three domains: spiritual/social, environmental, and financial. The pattern is consistent: structured information, real-time signals, institutional-grade governance.

## Active Projects

**Water & food stress intelligence for Kenya's 47 counties.**

Crisis-signal platform for NGOs, county governments, and farming communities. Composite indices (WSI/FSI/MSI/CRI) surface water stress, food production risk, and market pressure. ## Approach

The thread across all three projects: **opacity reduction at the point of decision.**

A priest determining fasting obligations, an NGO field worker assessing drought risk, a trader evaluating a setup — all face the same structural problem: consequential decision, limited time, incomplete information. These systems build the layer that makes the decision legible.

Technical choices follow from the users, not the other way around. SMS for farmers without smartphones. Role-gated web interfaces for coordinators. CSV audit trails for portfolio review. The stack serves the mission.

**Governance standards applied across all projects:**
- AGPL-3.0 or CC BY-NC-ND licensing with institutional deployment pathways
- DEMO/REAL data labeling on every user-facing output
- Documented methodology for every scoring algorithm
- Security policy, contributing guide, and CI/CD on every active repo
